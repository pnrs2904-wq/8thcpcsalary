Frequently Asked Questions

What is the expected 8th CPC fitment factor?+
No factor is officially notified yet. Public estimates range from about 1.92× to 2.86×, with some union demands quoted as high as 3.83×. The 7th CPC used 2.57×.
What is the current DA rate?+
DA is 60% effective 1 January 2026. The July 2026 revision to 63% is confirmed by AICPI-IW data, with Cabinet notification expected September–October 2026 and arrears from 1 July 2026.
When will the 8th CPC be implemented?+
The 8th CPC was constituted on 3 November 2025. As of August 2026 it has not submitted its recommendations. The revised pay is widely expected to take effect from 1 January 2026 retrospectively, with arrears, once notified.
How is 8th CPC salary calculated?+
New basic = (7th CPC basic + DA) × fitment factor. Add HRA (30/20/10% by city), Transport Allowance and other allowances for gross pay, then deduct NPS and income tax for in-hand salary.
